Last updated: May 2026
No data collected

TapPad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device — your sounds, your settings, your pads.

No tracking No analytics No ads Fully offline
Local storage only

Sound files and pad configurations are stored locally in your device's Documents directory using standard iOS file storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

In-app purchases

The Premium upgrade is processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information. Purchase history is managed by your Apple ID.

Microphone access

TapPad requests microphone access only when you choose to record a sound. Audio is saved locally and never leaves your device.
